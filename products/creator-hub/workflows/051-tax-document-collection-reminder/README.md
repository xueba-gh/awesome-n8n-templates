# Tax document collection reminder to clients

Accountants and bookkeepers: emails each client a checklist reminder until their documents are marked received.

## What you need

- Google Sheets
- Gmail

## Setup

1. Use a Google Sheet with a tab named **Client documents** and these headers in row 1: see `sheet-headers.csv`.
2. Open **Settings** and fill in your sheet URL, your email and names.
3. Connect Google Sheets and Gmail on the nodes with warnings.
4. Check the rule in **Find rows to act on** (it shows exactly which rows get an email) and the wording in **Send email**.
5. Run it once by hand with a test row, then switch the workflow to **Active**.

## Google Sheet columns (row 1)

`client_name`, `email`, `missing_documents`, `received`, `last_reminder`
