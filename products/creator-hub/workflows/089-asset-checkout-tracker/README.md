# Equipment checkout log with confirmation email

Staff log equipment they borrow. Everything is recorded in a sheet and they get a confirmation with the return date.

## What you need

- Google Sheets
- Gmail

## Setup

1. Make a Google Sheet with a tab named **Checkouts** and paste the header row from `sheet-headers.csv` (first row).
2. Open **Settings** and fill in your sheet URL, email and names.
3. Connect your accounts on every node with a warning (Google Sheets, Gmail).
4. Fill in the built-in form (open the form URL from the **Form** node).
5. Click **Execute workflow**, send a test, then switch the workflow to **Active**.

## Google Sheet columns (row 1)

`received_at`, `name`, `email`, `item`, `return_date`
