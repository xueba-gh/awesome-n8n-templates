# Keyword alerts from any RSS feed (jobs, tenders, listings)

Checks RSS feeds every 30 minutes and emails you each new item that contains your keywords, for job boards, tenders, property or deal feeds.

## What you need

- Gmail

## Setup

1. Open **Settings** and list your RSS feed URLs (most blogs have `/feed` or `/rss`; a YouTube channel is `https://www.youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml?channel_id=...`). Put the words to look for in `keywords` (any one match counts).
2. Connect Gmail on **Email me**.
3. Run it once by hand and look at **Keep new items**, then switch to **Active**.
