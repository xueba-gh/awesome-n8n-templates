# AI FAQ Chatbot Lite: website chat that answers from your Google Sheet

A website chatbot that answers customer questions only from the facts in your Google Sheet, with conversation memory.

**Free template.** Want it installed for you? I set up the full version on your own accounts. Message me through my n8n Creator profile.

## What it does
- Hosted chat page you can link to or embed
- Loads your FAQ sheet fresh on every message, so edits apply instantly
- Answers in 1 to 3 sentences, in the customer's language, and never invents prices or promises
- Gives your phone number and booking link when it doesn't know or the customer wants a person

## What you need
n8n, an OpenAI API key, a Google account (Sheets).

## Setup
1. Make a Google Sheet with a tab `Knowledge` and two headers: topic, answer. Add your real hours, prices, services and policies.
2. Open **Settings**: business name, booking link, phone number, sheet URL.
3. Connect credentials: OpenAI, Google Sheets.
4. Activate, open **Website chat** and copy the chat URL. Link to it from your site, or embed it.
